Delete your FiveTen account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and data from FiveTen (by FiveTen Studio, com.fivetenstudio.fiveten).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to fivetenstudio674@gmail.com with the subject "FiveTen account deletion".
  2. Include your player name as it appears on the leaderboard. If you can, add the approximate date you last played.
  3. We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your player account and random player ID
  • Your player name, chosen country and leaderboard scores
  • The link between your account and Google Play Games, if you signed in

What is kept

We keep no data about you after deletion. Settings stored only on your device are removed when you uninstall the game. Ad data collected by Google AdMob is handled by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
