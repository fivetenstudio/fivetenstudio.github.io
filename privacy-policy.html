Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game FiveTen (package com.fivetenstudio.fiveten), published by FiveTen Studio ("we", "us"), collects and how it is used. FiveTen does not ask for your email address, your real name, your contacts, your photos or your precise location.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhyWhere it is stored
A random player ID created by the gameTo keep your leaderboard accountMicrosoft PlayFab
The player name you chooseShown on the global leaderboardMicrosoft PlayFab

Google Play Games player ID and display name (only if you sign in with Google Play Games)To link your leaderboard account to your Google Play Games profile so it follows you across devicesGoogle; the link is stored in Microsoft PlayFab
Game settings (volume, effects, etc.)To remember your preferencesOnly on your device

2. Advertising

FiveTen shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown; if you do not consent, you may still see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with Options → Privacy choices in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings.

Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

3. Third-party services

  • Microsoft PlayFab (leaderboard and player accounts): privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
  • Google AdMob (ads): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in): policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the services listed above, only for the purposes described here.

4. What other players can see

Your chosen player name, country flag and score are visible to everyone on the global leaderboard. Please do not use your real full name or any personal information as your player name.

5. Data retention and deletion

Leaderboard data is kept while the game is active. To have your leaderboard entry and account data deleted, email us at fivetenstudio674@gmail.com with your player name (and, if possible, the date you last played). We will delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

6. Security

Data sent between the game and PlayFab, Google and AdMob is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

7. Children

FiveTen is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
